Ball Flight Lab

The GolfPhysics flight model (M2) against TrackMan tour and amateur data, handicap sniff tests and physical shape checks. Every shot is flown by the engine on flat ground in TrackMan's normalised air (sea level, 75 °F, 75 % humidity), no wind unless stated.

Distances in yards. Height axes are stretched so a 30-yard apex is readable; the scale is printed on each chart.
Built by cargo run --release -p golf-lab -- view from data/calibration/reference.v1.json and data/aero/ball.v1.json. Rows marked "unverified" are tour figures not yet checked against TrackMan's own table; they count half in the fit.
