# 04: Decision log

Every decision that shapes a contract, an invariant or a model, newest first. **Status:** `decided` (the owner
said so), `proposed` (written down, waiting on the owner), `superseded` (replaced; the entry says by what).

Add an entry **before** changing a contract or an invariant. Model tweaks inside a step don't need one; a change
to what a step consumes or produces does.
