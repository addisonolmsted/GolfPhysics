Gyro Orientation Check

By kind of swing

Medians over the swings in each group. "Error against gravity" is measured when the phone is next held still after the swing, where the raw accelerometer shows the true direction of down: an independent check of both methods' tilt.

Which is right after a fast swing?

Tilt error against gravity, by how fast the swing was. CoreMotion's error grows with speed; the raw gyro's doesn't.

raw gyroCoreMotion

Do they agree at impact?

The angle between the two orientations at the moment of impact, both starting from the same address. Close on slow swings; apart on fast ones.

The face twist agrees either way

The phone's rotation about its long axis between address and impact: the face opening or closing. CoreMotion's error is mostly tilt, so this angle comes out within a few degrees on both. Fades and draws separate clearly.
